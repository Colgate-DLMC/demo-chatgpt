About this catalog & its sources

Camera names and item records come from the DLMC resource-usage report covering October 1, 2020–October 1, 2026. Historical records do not establish that equipment is currently loanable. The booking report is historical and is not used to infer availability.

Specifications use the supplied DLMC DSLR + Lens Guide where available, with Canon documentation for the EOS 90D and Zoom documentation for the Q8n-4K. Missing specifications are shown as “Not documented.” Recommendations are editorial guidance based on camera type and documented specs. The 4K filter excludes models whose 4K capability has not been verified here.

Manual links come from the DLMC equipment-manuals index. Only matching model manuals are linked. Confirm older models and kit contents with staff.
